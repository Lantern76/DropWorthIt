# Sponsored listing policy

Projects can ask to be featured. Pricing is not set yet, and nothing is for sale today.

- Verification is mandatory before any listing, paid or not: official website, official X account, and an official announcement of the airdrop. The claim domain must be confirmed from the official site or X account.
- Paid placements always carry a "Sponsored" label in a distinct style that is never used for editorial picks.
- Payment never changes a verdict (Do it / Maybe / Skip), a risk score, or any fact on the page.
- We can decline or remove any listing, including paid ones, if facts can't be verified or the project turns out to be unsafe.
